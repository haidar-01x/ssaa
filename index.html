Happy Birthday, My Beloved Husband

To my darling, my love, and my beautiful blessing from Allah.

Alhamdulillah for the gift of your life and for the blessing of having you as my husband. Today, I celebrate not just the day you were born, but the wonderful man Allah has placed in my life.

My love, you are my companion, my comfort and one of the greatest blessings I get to call mine. I pray that Allah fills your new age with endless barakah, grants you good health, abundant rizq, beneficial knowledge and success in both this dunya and the Hereafter.

اللهم احفظه، وبارك في عمره ورزقه، واجعله من أهل الفردوس الأعلى.

O Allah, protect him, bless his life and provision, and make him among the people of the highest Paradise.

May Allah always guide your steps, ease your worries, answer your duas and grant you everything your heart wishes for that is good. May He make our marriage a source of tranquillity, affection and mercy, and may we continue to grow together in love and faith.

I pray that we remain each other's happiness in this world and companions in Jannatul Firdaus. I want to share countless beautiful moments with you, grow old beside you and keep choosing you through every season of life.

أنت نعمة أحمد الله عليها كل يوم.

You are a blessing I thank Allah for every day.

Happy Birthday, my sweetheart. May your days be filled with happiness, your heart with imaan and your life with Allah's endless mercy.

I love you dearly, today, tomorrow and in every beautiful chapter Allah writes for us.
